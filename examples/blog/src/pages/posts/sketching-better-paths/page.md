A rough map makes uncertainty easier to discuss. Before building a complicated workflow, I draw its simplest possible path:

```text
question → small experiment → observation → next question
```

The arrows matter more than the boxes. They reveal where information moves, where a decision waits, and where a shortcut might erase something useful.

## Leave room for revision

A sketch is deliberately incomplete. That makes it cheap to challenge and easy to redraw. The goal is not to predict every branch; it is to make the next decision legible.
