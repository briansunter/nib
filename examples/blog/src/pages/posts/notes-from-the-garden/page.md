The garden keeps a different calendar. Progress appears in small signals: a new leaf, damp soil, the first flower leaning toward the morning light.

## Three field notes

- **Conditions beat urgency.** Water and light matter more than willing a seed to grow.
- **Feedback can be quiet.** A curled edge often says enough.
- **Rest is part of the cycle.** Empty beds are preparing for what comes next.

The useful practice is to return, notice what changed, and respond with the smallest helpful action.
