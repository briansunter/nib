Attention is easier to protect when it has somewhere to land. I keep a small card beside my desk with one question:

> What would make the next hour feel well spent?

The answer does not need to be ambitious. Sometimes it is “finish the sketch.” Sometimes it is “walk without headphones.” The card simply turns an abstract intention into a visible choice.

![An abstract wash of violet and coral light](/images/field-notes.jpg)

## Try the smallest version

1. Choose one thing worth noticing.
2. Put it where your eyes already go.
3. Review what happened before choosing again.

This is not a system for squeezing more from a day. It is a way to remember that focus is something we can shape.
