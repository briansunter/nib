# French Laundry Baked Beets

From Thomas Keller's Masterclass. Simple beet recipe that concentrates the flavor and perfect tenderness.

10 minutes prep 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 450 g beets
- 15 ml avocado oil
- \- kosher salt
- 2 minced shallot
- \- Maldon salt
- 15 g balsamic vinegar
- 30 ml extra-virgin olive oil
- \- chives

## Equipment

- aluminum foil
- baking dish
- mixing bowl

## Instructions

1

Wash and dry the trimmed 450 g beets . Place the beets on aluminum foil with the shiny side of the foil up. Drizzle with 15 ml avocado oil .

2

Season with kosher salt , and fold the foil to form a pouch.

3

Place the pouch in a baking dish with the folded side up.

4

Bake at 350 °F for about 45 minutes . Test for doneness using a paring knife, looking for no resistance.

5

Beets easily stain cutting boards and hands, so peel while still warm under running water or wear gloves and work over parchment.

6

Cut beets into wedges

7

Put the beets in a mixing bowl . Add the 2 minced shallot to the beets, followed by Maldon salt , 15 g balsamic vinegar , 30 ml extra-virgin olive oil , and chives , reserving a sprinkle of chives.

8

Spoon the beets into a serving bowl, and finish with more chives. Optionally add blue cheese or gorgonzola crumbles
