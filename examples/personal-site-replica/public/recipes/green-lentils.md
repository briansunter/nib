# Whole Green Lentils with Garlic & Onion

A simple and flavourful method of cooking whole green lentils with garlic and onion. Serve with rice, vindaloo, or Gujarati-style green beans.

1 hour 5 minutes cook 10 minutes prep 1 hour 15 minutes total 5 servings

side lentils vegetarian indian stovetop

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp vegetable oil
- 0.50 tsp cumin seeds
- 4 garlic cloves
- 75 g onion
- 200 g whole green lentils
- 720 ml water
- 1 tsp salt
- 0.25 tsp cayenne pepper

## Equipment

- heavy pot

## Instructions

### Cook the Aromatics

1

Heat the 4 tbsp vegetable oil in a heavy pot over a medium flame. When hot, put in the 0.50 tsp cumin seeds . A few seconds later, put in the 4 garlic cloves , peeled and finely chopped. Stir and fry until the garlic pieces turn golden.

2

Now put in the 75 g onion , peeled and chopped. Stir and fry until the onion pieces begin to turn brown at the edges.

### Simmer the Lentils

3

Put in the 200 g whole green lentils , picked over, washed and drained, and the 720 ml water . Bring to the boil. Cover, reduce the heat to low and simmer for about 1 hour or until the lentils are tender.

### Season and Finish

4

Add the 1 tsp salt and 0.25 tsp cayenne pepper . Stir to mix and simmer gently for another 5 minutes .

Note Adjust the cayenne pepper to taste
