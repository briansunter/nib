# Oven Roasted Asparagus

Tender asparagus roasted with optional Parmesan and garlic, seasoned with sea salt and pepper, and finished with a splash of lemon juice.

14 min

side vegetarian asparagus

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 bunch asparagus
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 0.25 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 clove minced garlic
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 0.50 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

## Equipment

- oven
- mixing bowl

## Instructions

1

Preheat your oven to 425 °F (220°C).

2

Place 1 bunch asparagus , trimmed, into a mixing bowl . Drizzle with 3 tbsp olive oil and toss to coat. Sprinkle with 0.25 cup Parmesan cheese , 1 clove minced garlic , 1 tsp sea salt , and 0.50 tsp black pepper . Arrange asparagus in a single layer on a cookie sheet.

3

Bake in the preheated oven until just tender, 14 minutes depending on thickness.

4

Sprinkle with 1 tbsp lemon juice and serve.
