# Banana Pancakes

Fluffy banana pancakes made with ripe bananas, flour, and milk for a classic breakfast treat.

5 min

breakfast vegetarian

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 120 g flour
- 13 g white sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 g salt
- 3 egg
- 240 g milk
- 26 g avocado oil
- 3 bananas

## Instructions

1

Combine 120 g flour , 13 g white sugar , 2 tsp baking powder , and 1 g salt in a bowl. Mix together 3 egg , 240 g milk , 26 g avocado oil , and 3 bananas in a second bowl.

2

Stir flour mixture into banana mixture; batter will be slightly lumpy.

3

Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium high heat. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1/4 cup for each pancake. Cook until pancakes are golden brown, 5 minutes per side. Serve hot.
