# Healthy Breakfast

Avocado toast with cherry tomatoes and boiled eggs, served with fresh fruit.

5 minutes cook 10 minutes prep 15 minutes total 1 serving

## Ingredients

- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes
- 2 cups mixed fruit
- 3 slices whole grain bread
- 1 large avocado
- to taste salt
- to taste pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Equipment

- toaster

## Instructions

1

Place 2 large eggs in a pot of water, bring to a boil, then simmer 10 minutes for hard-boiled. Cool, peel, and slice.

2

Halve 1 cup cherry tomatoes .

3

Dice 2 cups mixed fruit (apple, banana, berries) into a bowl.

### Cook

4

Toast 3 slices whole grain bread to your preferred level of crispiness in a toaster .

### Assemble

5

Mash 1 large avocado in a bowl and season with to taste salt and to taste pepper .

6

Spread the mashed avocado evenly on each slice of toast.

7

Top with the halved cherry tomatoes and sliced boiled eggs.

8

Drizzle 1 tbsp olive oil over the toast.

Note Serve alongside the bowl of mixed fresh fruit.
