# Sous Vide Honey Garlic Salmon

Really easy, juicy, falvorful salmon in the sous vide.

via [amzn.to](https://amzn.to/3pWykyQ)

45 min

main sous-vide fish

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- \- salt
- \- pepper
- 4 cloves garlic
- \- lemon zest
- 0.25 cup honey
- 2 tbsp dijon mustard
- 1 tsp worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp melted butter

## Equipment

- Sous Vide

## Instructions

1

Fill and preheat the Sous Vide to 130 °F (54°C) for 45 minutes

2

Put fillets into each of two small cooking pouches and vacuum seal.

3

Drizzle the fillets with 1 tbsp olive oil , sprinkle with salt , pepper , 4 cloves garlic , and lemon zest .

4

Meanwhile, in a small bowl, combine the 0.25 cup honey , 2 tbsp dijon mustard , 1 tsp worcestershire sauce , and 1 tbsp melted butter . Set aside.

5

Remove the pouches and open them. Remove the fillets and pat them dry with paper towels.

6

Brush the fillets generously with the glaze and sear the exterior with a kitchen torch (or under a preheated broiler) just until the glaze bubbles.
