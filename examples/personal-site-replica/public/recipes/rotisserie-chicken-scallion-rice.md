## Ingredients

- 1½-2 cups rotisserie chicken
- \- chicken drippings
- 2 cups hot cooked rice
- 2 finely chopped green onions
- 1½ tablespoons four season scallion sauce
- 1 tablespoon golden shallot oil

## Equipment

- bowl

## About

A viral 5-minute hack, strips of shredded rotisserie chicken mixed into hot rice with chicken drippings, Four Season Scallion Sauce (Kai Dong), and golden shallot oil. Every spoonful is fragrant and savory. Works as breakfast, lunch, dinner, or a quick snack. Based on a recipe from Tiffy Cooks.

## Instructions

1

Shred 1½-2 cups rotisserie chicken into thin, bite-sized pieces using your hands or two forks. Reserve chicken drippings from the rotisserie bag.

### Assemble

2

Add 2 cups hot cooked rice to a bowl . Top with the shredded chicken and 2 finely chopped green onions .

3

Gently mix everything together so the rice absorbs the chicken juices.

### Season

4

Drizzle in 1½ tablespoons four season scallion sauce and 1 tablespoon golden shallot oil .

5

Mix until evenly coated. Taste and adjust if needed.

Note Optional: finish with a pinch of @white pepper{} and @sesame seeds{}.
