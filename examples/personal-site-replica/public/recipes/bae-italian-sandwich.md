# Bae Italian Sandwich

A mouthwatering Italian ciabatta sandwich packed with savory cold cuts, creamy mozzarella, tangy vinaigrette, and crunchy vegetables.

2 min 2 servings

sandwich italian lunch cold-cuts

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 0.50 tsp dried basil
- 0.25 cup extra virgin olive oil
- to taste salt
- to taste black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 6 ciabatta roll
- 8 oz fresh mozzarella
- 4 oz prosciutto
- 4 oz salami
- 4 oz capicola
- 0.50 cup Kalamata olives
- 0.50 cup banana peppers
- 1 medium tomato
- 1 small red onion
- to taste fresh basil leaves

## Equipment

- skillet
- panini press

## Instructions

### Vinaigrette

1

In a small bowl, whisk together the 2 tbsp red wine vinegar , 1 tsp Dijon mustard , 1 clove garlic (minced), 1 tsp dried oregano , and 0.50 tsp dried basil .

2

Slowly drizzle in the 0.25 cup extra virgin olive oil while continuously whisking to emulsify the dressing.

3

Season with to taste salt and freshly ground to taste black pepper . Set aside.

### Prepare the Ciabatta Rolls

4

Preheat a skillet or panini press over medium heat.

5

Lightly butter or brush 1 tbsp olive oil on the cut sides of each 2 ciabatta roll .

6

Toast the rolls in the skillet or panini press until golden and crispy. Remove from heat.

### Assemble the Sandwich

7

Drizzle a generous amount of the prepared vinaigrette over the bottom half of each 2 ciabatta roll .

8

Layer the 8 oz fresh mozzarella slices evenly over the vinaigrette.

9

Arrange the 4 oz prosciutto , 4 oz salami , and 4 oz capicola slices over the mozzarella, ensuring an even distribution of flavors.

Note You can substitute capicola with ham, turkey, or mortadella.

10

Sprinkle the sliced 0.50 cup Kalamata olives and 0.50 cup banana peppers over the cold cuts.

11

Add the 1 medium tomato slices and 1 small red onion slices for extra freshness and crunch.

12

Optionally, add to taste fresh basil leaves for an aromatic touch.

13

Drizzle a little more vinaigrette on the top half of the 2 ciabatta roll before placing it on top of the assembled ingredients.

### Press and Serve

14

Press the sandwich firmly to help meld the flavors. If using a panini press , grill for an additional 2-3 minutes to warm the ingredients together.

15

Slice the sandwich into halves or quarters for easy serving.
