# Simple Syrup

How to make traditional or rich simple syrup for drinks.

via [amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/Dishoom-cookbook-much-loved-Indian-restaurant/dp/1408890674?&_encoding=UTF8\&tag=bsunter-20\&linkCode=ur2\&linkId=9591a56da3cee0346dafbf6fd973e41c\&camp=1789\&creative=9325)

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 120 ml water
- 240 g white granulated sugar

## Equipment

- small saucepan
- funnel

## Instructions

1

Heat the 120 ml water in a small saucepan over medium-low heat until hot, but do not bring to a boil (190 degrees F).

2

Stir in the 240 g white granulated sugar (120 g for traditional syrup). Add the sugar and stir until fully dissolved.

3

Cool and store: Let the syrup cool to room temperature before using or storing. Using a funnel if needed, transfer the simple syrup into a glass jar and store in the refrigerator for up to 3 weeks.
