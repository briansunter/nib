# Air Fryer Cauliflower

Crispy, tender cauliflower florets roasted in the air fryer with paprika and cumin.

25 min 4 servings

vegetarian side dish air fryer quick cauliflower

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 head cauliflower
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 0.50 teaspoon cumin
- to taste salt
- to taste black pepper
- to taste fresh parsley

## Equipment

- bowl
- air fryer

## Instructions

1

Cut 1 head cauliflower into florets.

### Season

2

In a bowl , toss the cauliflower florets with 2 tablespoons olive oil , 1 teaspoon paprika , 0.50 teaspoon cumin , to taste salt , and to taste black pepper until well coated.

### Air Fry

3

Preheat the air fryer to 400 °F (200°C) for 5 minutes .

4

Arrange the cauliflower in the air fryer basket in a single layer. Air fry for 15-20 minutes , shaking the basket every 5 minutes , until the cauliflower is tender and crispy.

### Serve

5

Garnish with to taste fresh parsley , chopped, before serving.
