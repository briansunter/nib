## Instructions

### Beef

1

Season steak with salt and pepper . Cook in sous vide water bath at 140 °F for 1 hour .

### Seafood

2

Season salmon fillet with salt and lemon . Cook at 125 °F for 40 minutes .

### Vegetables

3
