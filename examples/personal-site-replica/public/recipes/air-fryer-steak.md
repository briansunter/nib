# Air Fryer Steak

Juicy ribeye or sirloin steaks cooked in the air fryer, seasoned with garlic powder, onion powder, and herbs.

48 min 2 servings

main steak air fryer dinner quick

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2 ribeye or sirloin steaks
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- to taste salt
- to taste black pepper
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme or rosemary
- to taste butter

## Equipment

- air fryer

## Instructions

### Prep the Steaks

1

Pat the 2 ribeye or sirloin steaks dry with paper towels. Brush both sides with 1 tablespoon olive oil .

2

Season both sides with to taste salt , to taste black pepper , 1 teaspoon garlic powder , 1 teaspoon onion powder , and 1 teaspoon dried thyme or rosemary . Let sit at room temperature for 10-15 minutes .

### Preheat Air Fryer

3

Preheat the air fryer to 400 °F (200°C) for 3-5 minutes .

### Cook the Steaks

4

Place the steaks in the air fryer basket in a single layer.

5

Air fry for 7-9 minutes for medium-rare, 10-12 minutes for medium, or 13-15 minutes for medium-well, flipping halfway through the cooking time.

Note Use a meat thermometer to check the internal temperature: 125°F (52°C) for medium-rare, 135°F (57°C) for medium, and 145°F (63°C) for medium-well.

Note For a more caramelized crust, sear the steaks briefly in a hot skillet after air frying.

### Rest the Steaks

6

Remove steaks from the air fryer and let them rest for 5 minutes to allow the juices to redistribute.

### Serve

7

Optionally, top each steak with to taste butter for extra flavor. Serve immediately with your favorite side dishes.

Note Adjust seasoning to your taste or try a steak seasoning blend.
