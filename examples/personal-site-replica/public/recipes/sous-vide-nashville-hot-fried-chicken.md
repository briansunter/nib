# Sous Vide Nashville Hot Chicken

Nashville Hot Chicken is deep fried chicken covered in a rich spicy oil served on a slice of white bread with pickles. The sous vide version makes it easier, better, and more consistent.

via [youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dHA3QHvELMc)

2h 49m

main sous-vide fried chicken southern

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 4 skin on boneless chicken thighs
- \- salt
- 2 tbsp msg
- \- peanut oil
- 450 g flour
- 16 g salt
- 475 ml buttermilk
- 2 tbsp hot sauce
- 2 eggs
- 25 g cayenne
- 11 g garlic powder
- 8 g smoked paprika
- 25 g dark brown sugar
- \- white bread
- \- pickle slices

## Equipment

- sous vide bag
- sous vide
- deep pot
- large bowl
- small bowl

## Instructions

1

Prepare a sous vide bag

2

Cover 4 skin on boneless chicken thighs with salt and 2 tbsp msg and seal in sous vide bag

3

Cook in sous vide for 2.5 hours at 167° F (75° C)

4

30 minutes before sous vide finishes, start preparing mixes listed below in 3 separate bowls.

5

Heat a deep pot or pan with peanut oil to 400 °F / 204°C.

6

For dry mix, whisk together 450 g flour (3 cups) and 10 g salt in large bowl

7

For wet mix, combine 475 ml buttermilk (2 cups), 2 tbsp hot sauce , and 2 eggs in large bowl

8

For spice topping, mix 25 g cayenne (2.5 tbsp) 11 g garlic powder (1 tbsp) 8 g smoked paprika (1 tbsp), 6 g salt (2 tsp), and 25 g dark brown sugar (2 tablespoons) in small bowl

9

Remove chicken from bag, save juices in container, for use in other recipes, if desired. Place chicken on cooling rack and pat dry.

10

Coat chicken in flour, shaking off excess, then coat in buttermilk, then back into the flour until evenly coated. Rest chicken on cooling rack for 15 minutes

11

Add chicken piece by piece (may need a couple batches) to deep bottom pot and fry until golden, 4 minutes . Remove from pot and rest on rack to drain.

12

Let oil cool down a bit and take 240 ml (1 cup) from the oil you used to fry the chicken. Mix in with spice mixture.

13

Place chicken on slice of white bread . Brush on hot oil and add pickle slices on top.
