# Marinated Chicken

Six chicken breast marinade variations, Greek Lemon, Cilantro Lime, Honey Mustard, Teriyaki, Fajita, and Italian.

## About

Each marinade is enough for 1 to 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken breast. Marinate at least 30 minutes or up to 8 hours.

## Instructions

### Chicken

1

Place 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast (2 to 3 breasts) into a sealable bag or glass container.

### Greek Lemon Marinade

2

Combine 0.25 cup olive oil , juice of lemon (about 3 tbsp lemon juice ), 2 garlic cloves minced, 2 tsp dried oregano , 1 tsp dried thyme , 1 tsp Dijon mustard , 1 tsp kosher salt , and 0.50 tsp black pepper in a medium bowl and whisk to combine.

### Cilantro Lime Marinade

3

Combine 0.25 cup olive oil , 0.25 cup cilantro leaves chopped, zest and juice of 2 limes (about 0.25 cup lime juice ), 2 garlic cloves minced, 1 tsp ground cumin , 0.50 tsp chili powder , 1 tsp kosher salt , and 0.50 tsp black pepper in a medium bowl and whisk to combine.

### Honey Mustard Marinade

4

Combine 3 tbsp honey , 3 tbsp Dijon mustard , 2 tbsp olive oil , 2 tsp apple cider vinegar , garlic clove minced, 1 tsp kosher salt , and 0.50 tsp black pepper in a medium bowl and whisk to combine.

### Teriyaki Marinade

5

Combine 0.25 cup tamari soy sauce , 1 tbsp sesame oil , 1 tbsp rice vinegar , green onion thinly sliced, 2 tsp fresh ginger minced (about 1/2-inch piece), and 2 garlic cloves minced in a medium bowl and whisk to combine.

### Fajita Marinade

6

Combine 0.25 cup olive oil , zest and juice of lime (about 2 tbsp lime juice ), garlic clove minced, 1 tsp chili powder , 1 tsp ground cumin , 0.50 tsp paprika , 1 tsp kosher salt , and 0.50 tsp black pepper in a medium bowl and whisk to combine.

### Italian Marinade

7

Combine 0.25 cup olive oil , 2 tbsp white wine vinegar , 2 tsp Italian seasoning , 2 garlic cloves minced, 0.50 tsp red pepper flakes , and 0.50 tsp kosher salt in a medium bowl and whisk to combine.

### Marinate

8

Pour the chosen marinade over the 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast in the sealable bag , seal, and refrigerate for 2 hours or at least 30 minutes .

### Cook

9

Remove chicken from marinade and cook as desired until internal temperature reaches 165F, about 20 minutes .
