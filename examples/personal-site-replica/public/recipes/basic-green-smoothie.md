# Basic Green Smoothie

Quick healthy green smoothie with frozen fruit, greens, and coconut water

5 minutes 1 serving

breakfast smoothie healthy quick

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 cup frozen fruit
- 2 cups greens
- 1 cup coconut water
- \- banana
- \- honey
- \- chia seeds
- \- nut butter

## Equipment

- blender

## Instructions

1

Add 1 cup frozen fruit and 2 cups greens to a blender .

2

Add 1 cup coconut water .

3

Add banana , a drizzle of honey , a spoonful of chia seeds , and a dollop of nut butter .

4

Blend until smooth.
