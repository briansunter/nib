# Chili Pomelo Salad

Hot, tangy, nutty, sweet salad with pomelo, kale, and dates

15 minutes 4 servings

salad indian vegetarian

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 20 g pistachio nuts
- 20 g pumpkin seeds
- 1 large pomelo
- 80 g kale
- 3 spring onions
- 6 Medjool dates
- 10 large fresh mint leaves
- a small handful coriander leaves
- 3 tsp chilli drizzle
- 1 tsp tamarind chutney

## Equipment

- bowl

## Instructions

1

Hot, tangy, nutty, sweet. Fresh kale gives this salad plenty of bite and vigour, while the red chilli adds an addictive heat. Mint for freshness, juicy bursts of pomelo, the sweetness of dates and crunch of pistachios

2

We serve this two ways: as a salad or side dish to share, or with cooled, pulled murgh malai. It makes an exceptional lunch on a hot day.

3

Combine 20 g pistachio nuts , 20 g pumpkin seeds , 1 large pomelo or large pink grapefruit, 80 g kale , thick stalks removed, finely shredded, 3 spring onions , 6 Medjool dates , 10 large fresh mint leaves , a small handful coriander leaves , 3 tsp chilli drizzle , 1 tsp tamarind chutney in a bowl and stir until evenly mixed.
