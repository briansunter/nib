# Air Fryer Garlic Broccolini

Quick and crispy broccolini tossed with garlic and olive oil, air fried until tender with slightly charred tips.

10 min 2 servings

vegetable air fryer quick side dish garlic

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2 bunch broccolini
- 1½ tablespoons olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
- \- lemon juice

## Equipment

- bowl
- air fryer

## Instructions

### Prepare the Broccolini

1

Rinse and pat dry the 1 bunch broccolini . Trim the tough ends.

### Season

2

In a bowl , toss the 1 bunch broccolini with 1½ tablespoons olive oil , 3 cloves garlic , ¼ teaspoon salt , ¼ teaspoon black pepper , and ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes .

### Preheat

3

Preheat the air fryer to 375 °F (190°C) for 3 minutes .

### Air Fry

4

Place the broccolini in a single layer in the air fryer basket. Cook for 7-10 minutes , shaking halfway through, until stems are tender and tops are slightly crispy.

### Serve

5

Transfer to a serving plate. Garnish with 1 teaspoon sesame seeds and a squeeze of lemon juice from lemon wedges.
