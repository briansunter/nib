# East Carolina BBQ Sauce

Thin, spicy, and vinegery BBQ sauce.

via [tasteofhome.com](https://www.tasteofhome.com/recipes/east-carolina-bbq-sauce/)

5 minutes prep

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 230 g cider vinegar
- 13 g brown sugar
- 6 g coarsely ground pepper
- 8 g hot pepper sauce
- 4 g salt
- 6 g crushed red pepper flakes

## Instructions

1

Combine 230 g cider vinegar (1 cup) 13 g brown sugar 6 g coarsely ground pepper (2 tsp) 8 g hot pepper sauce (2 tsp) 4 g salt (1 tsp) 6 g crushed red pepper flakes (1 tsp) in a jar or shaker.

2

Seal and shake until sugar has completely dissolved.

3

Use or store at room-temperature for up to 2 weeks.
