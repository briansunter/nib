# Beef Tacos

Quick beef tacos with taco kit, tomato, and lettuce

5 min 4 servings

mexican beef tacos quick

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 0.25 cup water
- \- taco shells
- \- diced tomato
- \- shredded lettuce

## Equipment

- skillet

## Instructions

1

Prepare 1 lb ground beef in a skillet over medium heat, breaking up and cooking until browned. Drain excess fat.

2

Stir in 1 packet taco seasoning and 0.25 cup water . Simmer for 5 minutes .

3

Warm taco shells according to package directions.

4

Fill taco shells with seasoned beef. Top with diced tomato and shredded lettuce .
