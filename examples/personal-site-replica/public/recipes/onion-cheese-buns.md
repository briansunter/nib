# Onion Cheese Buns

Soft, tender buns filled with a swirl of cheese, minced onions, and herbs.

24 minutes cook 20 minutes prep 3 hours 40 minutes total 12 servings

bread baking buns cheese onion

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 0.33 cup dried onions
- 0.33 cup hot water
- 1 tbsp pizza seasoning
- 0.33 cup cheese powder
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1 large egg
- 0.75 cup warm water
- 2 tsp instant yeast
- 1.50 tsp salt
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 0.25 cup dry milk
- 2-3 tbsp mozzarella cheese

## Equipment

- stand mixer
- bowl
- silicone mat
- baking sheet
- pan
- oven
- wire rack

## Instructions

### Filling

1

Soak 0.33 cup dried onions , minced, in 0.33 cup hot water while making the dough.

2

Just before shaping the dough, stir in 1 tbsp pizza seasoning , 0.33 cup cheese powder , and 1 tbsp butter , softened.

Note For fresh onion instead of dried: cook 3/4 cup finely diced onion with 1 tbsp butter over medium heat for 10-15 minutes until golden brown. Cool completely, then mix with remaining filling ingredients plus 3-4 tbsp water to make it spreadable.

### Dough

3

In a large mixing bowl or the bowl of a stand mixer , whisk together 1 large egg and 0.75 cup warm water .

4

Add 2 tsp instant yeast , 3 tbsp butter softened, 1.50 tsp salt , 3 cups all-purpose flour , and 0.25 cup dry milk to the bowl.

5

Stir to combine, then knead until smooth and elastic, 5-10 minutes .

6

Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl , turning to coat all sides. Cover with lightly greased plastic wrap or a lid and let rise until doubled in bulk, about 2 hours .

### Assembly

7

Turn the dough out onto a lightly greased work surface or silicone mat .

8

Pat, then roll the dough into an 18 " x 10 " rectangle.

9

Spread with the onion mixture, leaving 1 " free of filling along the long edge farthest from you.

10

Starting with the long edge closest to you, roll the dough into a log, pinching the seam closed.

11

Cut the log into 12 slices.

12

Place the buns on a greased baking sheet , leaving about 1 " between them. Or, for softer sides, place them close together in a lightly greased 9 " x 9 " pan . Cover and let rise until puffy, about 1 hour .

13

Sprinkle with 2-3 tbsp mozzarella cheese , shredded.

14

Towards the end of the rising time, preheat the oven to 350°F.

### Bake

15

Bake the buns for 22-26 minutes , or until golden brown.

16

Remove from the oven and cool on a wire rack .

Note Store leftover buns, well-wrapped, at room temperature for several days; freeze for longer storage.
