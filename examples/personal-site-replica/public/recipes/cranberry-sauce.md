# Cranberry Sauce

Fresh cranberry sauce with orange zest and cinnamon, cooked down to a jam-like consistency.

40 min 10 servings

sauce cranberry thanksgiving holiday side

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 12 oz fresh or frozen cranberries
- 1 cup sugar
- 0.50 cup water
- 2 strips orange zest
- 2 tbsp orange juice
- \- cinnamon stick
- 1 pinch kosher salt

## Equipment

- 3-quart saucier or saucepan
- wooden spoon

## Instructions

### Combine Ingredients

1

Add 12 oz fresh or frozen cranberries , 1 cup sugar , 0.50 cup water , 2 strips orange zest , 2 tbsp orange juice , cinnamon stick , and a pinch of 1 pinch kosher salt to a 3-quart saucier or saucepan .

### Cook

2

Set over medium-high heat and bring to a boil. Reduce to a simmer.

3

Stir occasionally as berries start to pop. Use a wooden spoon to press berries against the side of the pan, breaking them down.

4

Continue cooking until the mixture thickens to a jam-like consistency, approximately 10 minutes total.

Note Stir in water, 1 tablespoon at a time, until the desired consistency is achieved.

### Cool

5

Remove from heat and let cool for about 30 minutes .

### Serve or Store

6

Serve immediately or store in a sealed container.

Note Can be made up to a week ahead and stored in a sealed container in the refrigerator.

Note Can be frozen for several months. Defrost before serving.
