# Szechwan Chili Oil Dipping Sauce

Spicy Szechwan-style dipping sauce with ginger, garlic, soy, and chili oil

5 minutes 4 servings

sauce chinese dipping spicy

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 T chopped ginger
- 1 T chopped garlic
- 2 T soy sauce
- 2 T rice vinegar
- 1 t sugar
- 0.50 t white pepper
- 1 T chopped scallions
- 2 T chili oil

## Equipment

- bowl

## Instructions

### Mix

1

Combine 1 T chopped ginger , 1 T chopped garlic , 2 T soy sauce , 2 T rice vinegar , 1 t sugar , 0.50 t white pepper , 1 T chopped scallions , and 2 T chili oil in a small bowl .

2

Mix all ingredients together and serve as a dipping sauce for dumplings or spring rolls.
