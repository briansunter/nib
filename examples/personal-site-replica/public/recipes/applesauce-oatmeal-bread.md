# Applesauce Oatmeal Bread

A moist and flavorful oatmeal bread made with applesauce, spices, and walnuts. Great for breakfast with a smear of cream cheese.

60 minutes cook 10 minutes prep 1 hour 10 minutes total 18 servings

bread baking breakfast applesauce oatmeal

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 0.50 cup vegetable oil
- 1.50 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 0.50 cup whole wheat flour
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 0.25 teaspoon baking powder
- 0.50 teaspoon baking soda
- 1.50 teaspoons cinnamon
- 0.50 teaspoon allspice
- 0.25 teaspoon nutmeg
- 0.50 teaspoon table salt
- 0.50 cup rolled oats
- 0.75 cup applesauce
- 0.50 cup chopped walnuts

## Equipment

- oven
- loaf pan
- bowl

## Instructions

1

Preheat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease a 9 " x 5 " loaf pan .

2

In a large bowl , mix together 1 cup granulated sugar , 2 large eggs , 0.50 cup vegetable oil , and 1.50 teaspoons vanilla extract .

3

In a separate bowl , whisk together 0.50 cup whole wheat flour , 1 cup all-purpose flour , 0.25 teaspoon baking powder , 0.50 teaspoon baking soda , 1.50 teaspoons cinnamon , 0.50 teaspoon allspice , 0.25 teaspoon nutmeg , and 0.50 teaspoon table salt . Add the dry mixture to the wet ingredients and stir until just combined.

4

Fold in 0.50 cup rolled oats , 0.75 cup applesauce , and 0.50 cup chopped walnuts .

5

Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan . Bake for 55-60 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

Note A light sprinkling of oats on top of the batter before baking tells people about the goodness within.

6

Cool completely in the pan. Store well wrapped at room temperature for several days; freeze for longer storage.

Note You can substitute quick-cooking oats for old-fashioned rolled oats.
