# Asian-Style Oven-Baked Pork Belly

Crispy oven-baked pork belly with five-spice, soy, and hoisin glaze.

1 hour 15 minutes cook 20 minutes prep 1 hour 45 minutes total 4 servings

main pork oven asian dinner

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds pork belly
- \- pork belly dry with paper towels
- 2 tablespoons sea salt
- 1 tablespoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon five-spice powder
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons hoisin sauce
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 4 minced garlic cloves
- 1 inch piece%minced ginger
- \- pork belly
- 2 pork
- 2 chopped green onions

## Equipment

- oven
- wire rack
- roasting pan

## Instructions

### Prep the Pork Belly

1

Preheat your oven to 375°F.

2

Score the skin of the 2 pounds pork belly in a crosshatch pattern, being careful not to cut into the meat.

Note Scoring helps the fat render and the skin crisp up.

3

Pat the pork belly dry with paper towels .

### Season the Pork

4

In a small bowl, mix together the 2 tablespoons sea salt , 1 tablespoon black pepper , 1 tablespoon five-spice powder , 2 tablespoons soy sauce , 2 tablespoons hoisin sauce , 1 tablespoon rice vinegar , 1 tablespoon honey , 4 minced garlic cloves , and 1 inch piece%minced ginger .

5

Rub the mixture all over the pork belly , ensuring it is well coated, especially in the scored areas.

### Bake the Pork Belly

6

Place the pork belly on a wire rack set in a roasting pan , skin side up.

7

Roast in the oven for 75 minutes , or until the skin is golden and crispy.

Note If the skin is not crisping up, switch to broil for the last 5-10 minutes, but keep a close eye on it to avoid burning.

### Rest and Serve

8

Remove the pork belly from the oven and let rest for 10-15 minutes before slicing.

9

Garnish with 2 chopped green onions and serve with steamed rice, pickled vegetables, or a simple salad.
