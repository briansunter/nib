# Herbal Compound Butter

Softened butter blended with parsley, garlic, lemon juice, and fresh herbs, a versatile finish for steaks, vegetables, or bread.

10 minutes 8 servings

butter herbs condiment

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 stick butter
- 1.50 tsp lemon juice
- 0.50 tsp salt
- 1 clove garlic
- 3 tbsp fresh parsley
- 1.50 tbsp fresh chopped herbs

## Equipment

- mixing bowl

## Instructions

1

Combine softened 1 stick butter (1/2 cup), 1.50 tsp lemon juice , 0.50 tsp salt (or to taste), minced 1 clove garlic , chopped 3 tbsp fresh parsley , and 1.50 tbsp fresh chopped herbs (basil, oregano, rosemary, etc.) in a mixing bowl and mix until well blended.
