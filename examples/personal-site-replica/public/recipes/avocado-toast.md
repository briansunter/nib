# Avocado Toast

Simple avocado toast. The most important things are a hearty slice of bread and perfectly ripe avocados.

via [amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/Joshua-Weissman-Unapologetic-Cookbook/dp/1615649980?&_encoding=UTF8\&tag=bsunter-20\&linkCode=ur2\&linkId=5300e43d60cfb8b95202ef018574abce\&camp=1789\&creative=9325)

5 mins cook 10 mins prep 1 serving

snack vegetarian

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 slice bread
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp melted butter
- 1 clove garlic
- \- avocado
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 pinch salt
- \- chili oil
- 1 handful basil

## Equipment

- toaster

## Instructions

1

Toast the 1 slice bread either in a toaster or in a pan with 1 tbsp butter .

2

Spread 1 tbsp melted butter on toast.

3

Cut the 1 clove garlic in half, and rub the cut sides all over each slice of toast to perfume with garlic.

4

Mash the avocado into chunks.

5

Mix splash of 1 tbsp lime juice and 1 pinch salt into avocado mixture.

6

Spread avocado on the toast.

7

Optionally, drizzle with chili oil .

8

Add a generous pinch of flaky sea salt.

9

Tear 1 handful basil and sprinkle on top.
