# No-Cook Teriyaki Sauce

Quick homemade teriyaki sauce with sake, mirin, soy, and oyster sauce

5 minutes 4 servings

sauce japanese teriyaki

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 0.25 cup sake
- 0.25 cup mirin
- 0.25 cup soy sauce
- 0.25 cup oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon sugar

## Equipment

- bowl

## Instructions

1

Combine 0.25 cup sake , 0.25 cup mirin , 0.25 cup soy sauce , 0.25 cup oyster sauce , and 1 tablespoon sugar in a bowl .

2

Whisk together all the ingredients until the sugar is dissolved.

3

Store in the fridge for up to a couple weeks.
