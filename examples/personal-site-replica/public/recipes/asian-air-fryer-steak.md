# Asian Steak Stir Fry in the Air Fryer

Asian-style steak stir fry cooked in the air fryer with a savory sweet sauce.

15 minutes cook 30 minutes prep 45 minutes total 4 servings

main steak air fryer asian stir fry dinner

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 lb flank steak
- 0.50 cup cornstarch
- 0.25 tsp baking soda
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 3 tsp minced ginger
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- \- oil spray
- 2 tsp oil
- 4 cloves garlic
- 0.50 cup soy sauce
- 0.50 cup water
- 1.25 cup brown sugar
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1.50 tsp chili garlic sauce
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp cold water
- \- green onions
- \- sesame seeds

## Equipment

- air fryer
- air fryer basket
- saucepan

## Instructions

### Steak

1

Slice 1 lb flank steak (or sirloin) into thin strips about 1/4 to 1/2 inch thick, against the grain.

2

Toss with 0.25 cup cornstarch and 0.25 tsp baking soda (optional, for tenderizing). The mixture will be slightly sticky.

### Marinade

3

In a bowl, combine 2 tbsp soy sauce , 2 tsp sesame oil , 1 tbsp minced garlic , 1 tsp minced ginger , and 1 tsp brown sugar .

4

Toss with the sliced steak and marinate for at least 30 minutes , up to 2 hours .

### Cook the Steak

5

Preheat the air fryer to 400 F (200C) for 3 minutes .

6

After marinating, toss steak with 0.25 cup cornstarch until evenly coated.

7

Lay steak strips flat in the air fryer basket . Spray generously with oil spray .

8

Cook for 7 minutes to 10 minutes , shaking the basket every 2 minutes to 3 minutes . Work in batches to avoid overcrowding.

### Sauce

9

Heat 2 tsp oil in a saucepan over medium heat.

10

Add 4 cloves garlic (minced) and 2 tsp minced ginger ; saute for 30 seconds .

11

Add 0.50 cup soy sauce (low sodium preferred), 0.50 cup water , 0.50 cup brown sugar to 0.75 cup brown sugar , 2 tbsp rice vinegar (optional), and 0.50 tsp chili garlic sauce to 1 tsp chili garlic sauce (adjust to taste).

12

Bring to a gentle boil.

13

Stir in 2 tsp cornstarch dissolved in 2 tbsp cold water and simmer until thickened, 1 minute to 2 minutes .

### Combine and Serve

14

Transfer cooked steak to a bowl. Toss with the sauce and let sit for 5 minutes .

15

Garnish with green onions and sesame seeds . Serve over rice or with your favorite vegetables.

Note Thinly sliced vegetables like carrots, onions, and bell peppers make great additions.
