## About

An easy and delicious creamy potato soup made in just one pot. Loaded with bacon and creamy potato goodness.

## Instructions

### Cook Bacon

1

Place 6 strips bacon in a dutch oven or soup pot over medium heat and cook until bacon is crisp and browned.

2

Remove bacon pieces and set aside, leaving the fat in the pot.

### Build the Base

3

Add 3 tablespoons butter and yellow onion and cook over medium heat until onions are tender ( 3-5 minutes ).

4

Add 3 cloves garlic and cook until fragrant ( 30 seconds ).

5

Sprinkle 0.33 cup all-purpose flour over the ingredients in the pot and stir until smooth, using a whisk if needed.

### Simmer

6

Add 2.50 lbs gold potatoes to the pot along with 4 cups chicken broth , 2 cups milk , 0.67 cup heavy cream , 1.50 teaspoons salt , 1 teaspoon ground pepper , and 0.25 teaspoon ancho chili powder . Stir well.

7

Bring to a boil and cook until potatoes are tender when pierced with a fork ( 10 minutes ).

### Puree

8

Reduce heat to simmer. Remove approximately half of the soup to a blender (be careful, it will be hot) and puree until smooth. Alternatively, use an immersion blender .

Note I prefer potato soup with chunks of potato, puree just half. For completely creamy soup, puree all of it in batches.

9

Return the pureed soup to the pot and add 0.67 cup sour cream and the reserved bacon pieces. Stir well.

10

Allow soup to simmer for 15 minutes before serving.

### Serve

11

Top with shredded cheddar cheese , chives , sour cream , and bacon .
