# Thickened Teriyaki Sauce

Teriyaki Sauce

3 min 4 servings

sauce teriyaki japanese condiment

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 0.50 cup soy sauce
- 0.25 cup brown sugar
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger
- 2 tbsp honey
- 0.25 cup mirin
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp water

## Equipment

- saucepan
- bowl

## Instructions

Note Ready in 10 minutes, no junk ingredients, and crazy delicious. You'll never use store-bought again!

1

Combine 0.50 cup soy sauce , 0.25 cup brown sugar , minced 3 cloves garlic , grated 1 tbsp fresh ginger , 2 tbsp honey , and 0.25 cup mirin in a saucepan .

2

Heat over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves.

3

In a small bowl , whisk 1 tbsp cornstarch and 2 tbsp water together to make a slurry. Add to the saucepan.

Note To use as a marinade, simply omit the cornstarch, let the sauce cool completely, and you've got the perfect teriyaki marinade!

4

Continue cooking and stirring for 3 minutes until the sauce thickens.

5

Remove from heat and let cool. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
