# King Arthur Pizza Dough

A simple and versatile pizza dough recipe

2h 50m

pizza baking dough

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2 tsp instant yeast
- 1 pinch sugar
- 2 tbsp water
- 360 g King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1.25 tsp table salt
- 198 g water

## Equipment

- stand mixer
- bowl

## Instructions

1

Mix together active dry yeast or 2 tsp instant yeast with a pinch of 1 pinch sugar in lukewarm 2 tbsp water . Let the mixture sit at room temperature for 15 minutes , until it bubbles and expands. If using instant yeast, this step can be skipped.

2

Weigh or measure 360 g King Arthur Unbleached All-Purpose Flour by gently spooning it into a cup and sweeping off any excess. Combine the yeast mixture (or the instant yeast) with 2 tbsp olive oil , 1.25 tsp table salt , and 198 g water - 250g. Adjust water quantity based on humidity and climate.

3

Mix and knead everything together by hand, stand mixer , or bread machine set on the dough cycle, until a soft, smooth dough forms. In a stand mixer, this should take 5 minutes at second speed.

4

Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl , cover the bowl, and allow it to rise till it's very puffy. This will take about 60 minutes using instant yeast, or 90 minutes using active dry yeast. If it takes longer, allow extra time.
