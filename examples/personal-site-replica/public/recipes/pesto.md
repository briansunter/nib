# Pesto

Simple homemade pesto

via [cooking.nytimes.com](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/2653-basic-pesto)

sauce italian

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 20 g basil leaves
- 20 g pine nuts
- 3 cloves garlic
- 50 g olive oil
- 60 g fresh grated parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1

Combine 20 g basil leaves , 20 g pine nuts (or walnuts) and 3 cloves garlic in a food processor and process until very finely minced.

2

With the machine running slowly dribble in 50 g olive oil and process until the mixture is smooth.

3

Add 60 g fresh grated parmesan cheese and process very briefly, just long enough to combine.

4

Store in refrigerator or freezer.
