# Old-Fashioned Pumpkin Pie

A timeless pumpkin pie with creamy spiced filling and flaky buttery crust, perfect for Thanksgiving or any fall gathering.

55 minutes cook 1 hour prep 1 hour 55 minutes total 8 servings

dessert pumpkin pie thanksgiving fall baking

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1.75 tsp kosher salt
- 0.75 cup shortening
- 6 tbsp ice water
- 1.13 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- 1.50 tsp ground ginger
- 0.50 tsp ground cloves
- 3 large eggs
- 15 oz canned pumpkin puree
- 1.13 cup evaporated milk
- \- whipped cream
- \- ice cream

## Equipment

- oven
- pie pan
- wire rack

## Instructions

### Pie Dough

1

In a large bowl, whisk together the 2 cup all-purpose flour (plus extra for dusting), 2 tsp ground cinnamon , and 1 tsp kosher salt .

2

Using a pastry blender or two knives, cut the 0.75 cup shortening (cubed) into the flour mixture until it resembles pea-sized crumbs.

3

Gradually add the 6 tbsp ice water , 1 tablespoon at a time, stirring gently until the dough comes together.

4

Transfer the dough to a work surface lined with plastic wrap. Shape it into a disc, wrap tightly, and refrigerate for 30 minutes .

### Filling

5

Preheat the oven to 425 F (220 C).

6

In a small bowl, mix together the 1.13 cup sugar (1 cup plus 2 tablespoons), 1 tbsp ground cinnamon (plus a pinch), 1.50 tsp ground ginger , 0.50 tsp ground cloves , and 0.75 tsp kosher salt .

7

In a large bowl, whisk the 3 large eggs . Add the 15 oz canned pumpkin puree and spice mixture, whisking until smooth. Gradually whisk in the 1.13 cup evaporated milk (1 cup plus 2 tablespoons) until fully combined.

### Roll Out the Dough

8

Once chilled, roll the dough on a lightly floured surface into a 0.25-inch-thick (6mm) round. Keep the surface and rolling pin floured to prevent sticking.

9

Transfer the dough to a 9.5-inch (24cm) pie pan . Tuck the edges under and crimp.

Note Handle the dough as little as possible for the flakiest crust.

### Assemble and Bake

10

Pour the filling into the prepared pie shell.

11

Bake for 15 minutes , then reduce the oven temperature to 350 F (180 C) and bake for an additional 40 minutes , or until the center jiggles slightly.

### Cool and Serve

12

Cool the pie on a wire rack for 2 hours .

13

Slice and serve with whipped cream or ice cream .
