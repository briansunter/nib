# Classic Banana Bread

A moist and flavorful bread made with ripe bananas, perfect for breakfast or a snack.

70 minutes cook 20 minutes prep 90 minutes total 1 serving

breakfast banana bread baking snack sweet

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- \- 9x5-inch loaf pan
- \- parchment paper
- 1.75 cups all-purpose flour
- 0.50 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 0.50 cup unsalted butter
- 1 cups granulated sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3 ripe bananas
- 0.50 cup sour cream
- 0.50 cup chopped walnuts
- 0.50 cup chocolate chips

## Equipment

- oven
- bowl
- wire rack

## Instructions

### Prepare Oven and Pan

1

Preheat your oven to 350 F. Grease your 9x5-inch loaf pan or line it with parchment paper .

### Mix Dry Ingredients

2

In a medium bowl , whisk together 1.75 cups all-purpose flour , 0.50 teaspoon salt , and 1 teaspoon baking soda . Set aside.

### Cream Butter and Sugar

3

In a large bowl , beat 0.50 cup unsalted butter (softened) and 1 cups granulated sugar together until light and fluffy, about 3 minutes .

### Add Eggs and Vanilla

4

Beat in 2 eggs one at a time, then stir in 1 teaspoon vanilla extract .

### Incorporate Bananas and Sour Cream

5

Mix in the 3 ripe bananas (mashed) and 0.50 cup sour cream until well combined.

### Combine with Dry Ingredients

6

Gradually add the dry ingredients to the banana mixture, stirring until just combined.

Note Avoid overmixing the batter or the bread will be tough.

### Add Optional Ingredients

7

Fold in 0.50 cup chopped walnuts or 0.50 cup chocolate chips if using.

### Bake

8

Pour the batter into the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top. Bake in the preheated oven for 65 minutes , or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Cool and Serve

9

Let the banana bread cool in the pan for 10 minutes , then transfer it to a wire rack to cool completely. Slice and enjoy.
