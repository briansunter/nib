## Ingredients

- \- 425 degrees
- 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 3 tablespoons spicy seasoning mix
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 sweet potatoes
- 5 cups broccoli florets
- 2 olive oil
- \- coarse sea salt
- \- freshly cracked pepper
- \- avocado
- \- hummus
- \- lemon juice
- \- chives

## About

Great for weekly meal prep, divide into containers for ready-to-go meal starters.

## Instructions

### Marinate Chicken

1

Preheat the oven to 425 degrees .

2

Toss 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken breasts with 3 tablespoons spicy seasoning mix and a quick stream of 3 tablespoons olive oil . Stir to combine and store in the fridge for about 30 minutes while you prep other ingredients.

### Prep Sheet Pans

3

Arrange 3 sweet potatoes on a sheet pan, preferably one with edges to catch drips.

4

Arrange 5 cups broccoli florets on a separate sheet pan.

5

Drizzle vegetables with olive oil and sprinkle generously with coarse sea salt and freshly cracked pepper .

6

Arrange the chicken on a separate sheet pan.

### Bake

7

Bake all ingredients for 12-15 minutes . Remove the broccoli and chicken. Stir sweet potatoes and roast for 15 minutes or so.

### Serve

8

Divide servings into containers. Serve with avocado / hummus / lemon juice / chives / olive oil .
