# Secret Burger Sauce

Quick five-minute mayo-based burger sauce, Epicurious's not-so-secret recipe.

5 minutes 8 servings

sauce condiment burger

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 0.25 cup mayonnaise
- 1.50 tsp spicy pickle juice
- 1.50 tsp ketchup
- 1 tsp yellow mustard
- 0.25 tsp smoked paprika
- 0.25 tsp garlic powder
- 0.25 tsp onion powder

## Equipment

- bowl

## About

Epicurious Not-So-Secret Sauce. Yields about 1/3 cup. Active and total time: 5 minutes.

## Instructions

1

Combine 0.25 cup mayonnaise , 1.50 tsp spicy pickle juice , 1.50 tsp ketchup , 1 tsp yellow mustard , 0.25 tsp smoked paprika , 0.25 tsp garlic powder , and 0.25 tsp onion powder in a small bowl and stir until well mixed.

2

Refrigerate until ready to use.
