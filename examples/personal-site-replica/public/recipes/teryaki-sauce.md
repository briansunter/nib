# Japanese Restaurant-Style Teriyaki Sauce

Teryaki Sauce

via [thespruceeats.com](https://www.thespruceeats.com/japanese-restaurant-style-teriyaki-sauce-recipe-2031569)

10 min 4 servings

sauce teriyaki japanese condiment

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 0.50 cup soy sauce
- 0.25 cup mirin
- 0.25 cup sake
- 4 tbsp sugar

## Equipment

- saucepan

## Instructions

1

Combine 0.50 cup soy sauce , 0.25 cup mirin , 0.25 cup sake , and 2 tbsp sugar in a saucepan .

2

Bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring until the 2 tbsp sugar dissolves.

3

Continue simmering for 10 minutes until the sauce reduces and thickens slightly.

4

Remove from heat and let cool. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator.
