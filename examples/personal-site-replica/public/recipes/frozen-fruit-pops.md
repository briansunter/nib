# Frozen Fruit Pops

Orange mango coconut popsicles

4 hours 6 servings

dessert frozen fruit popsicle

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2 cups mango
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 3 tablespoons orange juice
- 3 tablespoons honey

## Equipment

- popsicle molds

## Instructions

### Orange Mango Coconut Popsicles

1

Blend 2 cups mango with 1 cup coconut milk and 3 tablespoons orange juice until smooth.

2

Pour into popsicle molds . Add 3 tablespoons honey to taste.

3

Freeze for at least 4 hours until solid.

4

Run molds under warm water briefly to release.
