# All Purpose Barbecue Sauce

Good general purpose sweet bbq sauce

via [amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/Joshua-Weissman-Unapologetic-Cookbook/dp/1615649980?&_encoding=UTF8\&tag=bsunter-20\&linkCode=ur2\&linkId=5300e43d60cfb8b95202ef018574abce\&camp=1789\&creative=9325)

15 minutes prep

sauce southern

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 108 g brown sugar
- 272 g ketchup
- 63 g molasses
- 60 ml white distilled vinegar
- 10 g garlic powder
- 7 g smoked paprika
- 4 g cayenne pepper
- 15 ml Worcestershire sauce
- 18 g kosher salt
- 3 g black pepper

## Equipment

- medium saucepan
- bowl
- smoking gun
- airtight container

## Instructions

1

In a medium saucepan , stir together 108 g brown sugar , 272 g ketchup , 63 g molasses , 60 ml white distilled vinegar , 10 g garlic powder , 7 g smoked paprika , 4 g cayenne pepper , 15 ml Worcestershire sauce , 18 g kosher salt , and 3 g black pepper .

2

Place over medium heat, and let the mixture come to a simmer.

3

Once simmering, continue cooking, stirring occasionally, for 8 minutes , or until reduced and thickened slightly.

4

Pour the barbecue sauce into a bowl . If you have a smoking gun , cover the bowl with plastic wrap and slide the probe of the gun into the bowl. Fill the bowl with smoke, and remove the probe. Let sit, covered, for 5 minutes . Stir, taste, and repeat 2 or 3 more times until smoky to your liking.

5

Serve how you like it! Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 month.
