# Potatoes with Sesame Seeds

Easy, delicious Til Ke Aloo, waxy potatoes stir-fried with cumin, mustard, and sesame seeds. Pairs well with Indian meals or simple roasted and grilled meats.

8 min 6 servings

side potato indian vegetarian

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 kg waxy potatoes
- 6 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 tsp black mustard seeds
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds
- 2 tsp salt
- 0.50 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

## Equipment

- frying-pan

## Instructions

1

Boil the 1 kg waxy potatoes in their jackets. Drain and cool them for 3-4 hours. Peel the potatoes and chop them into 2 cm cubes.

### Cook

2

Heat the 6 tbsp vegetable oil in a 25-30cm frying-pan over a medium flame. When the oil is very hot, put in the 2 tsp cumin seeds , 2 tsp black mustard seeds and 2 tbsp sesame seeds . As soon as the seeds begin to pop, put in the chopped potatoes. Stir and fry the potatoes for about 5 minutes .

Note A non-stick or well-seasoned cast-iron frying pan works best.

Note Look for a few brown spots on the potatoes for best flavor.

### Season

3

Add the 2 tsp salt , 0.50 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder and 1 tbsp lemon juice . Stir and fry for another 3 minutes .
