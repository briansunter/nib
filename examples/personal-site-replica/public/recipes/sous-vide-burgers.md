# Sous Vide Burgers

Sous Vide Burgers

3h 10m 4 servings

sous-vide burgers beef

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 24 to 30 oz freshly ground beef chuck
- \- kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 4 slices cheese slices

## Equipment

- sous vide immersion circulator
- skillet

## About

Burgers cooked sous-vide are extremely juicy and evenly cooked.

## Instructions

1

Preheat sous vide immersion circulator to desired temperature (130 °F for medium).

2

Divide 24 to 30 oz freshly ground beef chuck into 4 portions (6 oz each) and form into patties. Season generously with kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper .

3

Place patties in vacuum bags and seal. Cook for 40 minutes to 2.5 hours .

4

Remove from bags and pat dry. Heat 1 tbsp vegetable oil in a skillet over high heat. Sear burgers for 30 seconds per side. Add 4 slices cheese slices if desired and cover to melt.

5

Serve on buns with desired toppings.
