# Sous Vide Fajita Vegetables

Sous Vide Fajita Vegetables

via [savoringthegood.com](https://www.savoringthegood.com/sous-vide-fajita-vegetables/)

15 min 4 servings

sous-vide fajita vegetables peppers onion

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- \- purple onion
- \- white onion
- 3 bell peppers
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- \- salt and pepper

## Equipment

- sous vide immersion circulator

## Instructions

1

Preheat sous vide immersion circulator to 183°F.

2

Slice the purple onion , white onion , and 3 bell peppers into 1/2 inch slices.

3

Place sliced vegetables in a vacuum bag with 1 tbsp olive oil and salt and pepper . Seal according to manufacturer's instructions.

4

Cook in the water bath for 15 minutes until tender-crisp.

5

Remove from bag and serve as fajita filling or alongside your favorite protein.
