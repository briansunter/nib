# Fresh Lime Soda

Simple lime soda that's much better than anything artificial.

## Ingredients

- 30 ml simple syrup
- 20 ml freshly squeezed lime juice
- 240 ml soda water
- \- lime slice

## Instructions

1

Make simple syrup by first boiling together equal parts sugar and water, then letting it cool.

2

Fill a glass with ice cubes, then add 30 ml simple syrup (2 tbsp) and 20 ml freshly squeezed lime juice (1.5 tbsp) (use regular supermarket persian limes).

3

Add 240 ml soda water (1 cup).

4

Stir well, then serve garnished with a lime slice .
