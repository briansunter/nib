## Ingredients

- 14.50 oz diced tomatoes
- 0.25 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 0.25 cup fresh basil
- 6 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp white balsamic vinegar
- 0.50 tsp salt
- 0.25 tsp cayenne pepper
- 2 lb shrimp

## Equipment

- food processor
- glass dish
- grill
- grill pan

## About

A quick and flavorful marinade for shrimp. Reducing the macros for the marinade ingredients by half is conservative, you're probably not consuming half of that EVOO, but 50% is a safe number. Round out your fat requirements with a few nuts or a drizzle of additional EVOO. Awesome served with steamed rice.

## Instructions

### Marinade

1

Combine 14.50 oz diced tomatoes , 0.25 cup extra virgin olive oil , 0.25 cup fresh basil , 6 cloves garlic , 1 tbsp white balsamic vinegar , 0.50 tsp salt , and 0.25 tsp cayenne pepper in a food processor . Process until smooth.

2

Add 2 lb shrimp and marinade to a glass dish . Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 1 hour.

### Grill

3

Preheat a sprayed grill or lightly oiled grill pan over medium heat.

4

Discard excess marinade. Cook shrimp 3 minutes per side.
