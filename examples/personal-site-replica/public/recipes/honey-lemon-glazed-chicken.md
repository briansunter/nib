## About

A bright, craveable dish, juicy boneless chicken thighs pan-fried until golden, then coated in a glossy honey lemon glaze made with fresh lemon juice and zest. Like Chinese takeout lemon chicken but with thicker, juicier cuts. Great over rice or as a protein topping for salads. Based on a recipe from Tiffy Cooks.

## Instructions

### Marinate the Chicken

1

In a bowl , combine 4 boneless chicken thighs , 2 tablespoons soy sauce , 1 tablespoon vegetarian oyster sauce , 1 teaspoon garlic powder , ½ teaspoon salt , ½ teaspoon black pepper , and 2 tablespoons cornstarch . Mix until the chicken is evenly coated.

### Make the Lemon Glaze

2

In a small bowl , mix 1-2 tablespoons lemon zest , ¼ cup fresh lemon juice , 2 tablespoons honey , ¼ teaspoon salt , and 1 teaspoon cornstarch . Set aside.

### Pan-Fry

3

Heat 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Pan-fry the chicken for 5 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Remove and set aside.

### Glaze

4

Pour the lemon sauce into the pan and simmer for 30 seconds until glossy.

5

Add the chicken back in and cook over medium-high heat for 2-3 minutes , tossing to coat evenly.

### Serve

6

Serve the chicken thighs whole or cut into bite-sized slices over steamed rice . Garnish with cilantro and lemon slices .
