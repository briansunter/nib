# Broccoli Almond Soup

A healthy broccoli almond soup, light and nutritious, great for weight loss meals.

20 minutes cook 10 minutes prep 30 minutes total 4 servings

soup broccoli almonds healthy weight-loss vegetarian

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 4 cups broccoli florets
- 0.25 cup whole almonds
- 1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic
- \- onion
- to taste salt
- 1 cup milk
- \- whole black peppercorns

## Equipment

- wok
- pan
- blender
- saucepan

## Instructions

1

Wash the 2 cups broccoli florets and prepare the rest of the ingredients.

2

Soak 0.25 cup whole almonds in hot water for about 10 minutes . Peel off the skin from the almonds and set aside.

### Cook

3

Heat 1 tsp extra virgin olive oil in a wok over medium heat. Add 4 cloves garlic , finely chopped, and onion , sliced, and saute until the onions have softened, about 1 minute .

4

Once the onions have softened, add the 2 cups broccoli florets and saute for about 1 minute . Sprinkle to taste salt , cover the pan and steam cook the broccoli for about 3-4 minutes .

Note Do not overcook the broccoli, it should still have a slight bite.

5

Once the broccoli has steamed just enough to take a bite, turn off the heat. Allow the broccoli onion mixture to cool.

### Blend

6

Once cooled, add the broccoli, soaked almonds and 1 cup milk into a blender . Blend to make a smooth puree.

### Finish

7

Pour the broccoli almond soup into a saucepan and bring it to a brisk boil, then turn off the heat.

Note Do this step only when you are ready to serve the soup.

8

Check the salt and adjust according to taste.

### Serve

9

Garnish the Broccoli Almond Soup with whole black peppercorns , coarsely pounded, and serve hot.

10

Serve along with Garlic Bread With Herb Butter, Zucchini Salad Recipe With Thai Hot Chili Dressing, and Spaghetti with Pesto and Parmesan for a delicious weekend dinner.
