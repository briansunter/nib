# Mughlai Saag

Spinach with onions, a Mughlai-style saag dish.

18 min 4 servings

side spinach indian vegetarian

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2 kg spinach
- 220 gram onions
- 4 tbsp ghee
- \- green chilli
- 1 tsp fresh ginger
- 1 tsp salt
- 0.50 tsp sugar
- 125 ml water
- 0.25 tsp garam masala

## Equipment

- pot

## Instructions

1

Cut the 1 kg spinach (washed and trimmed) across into 1 cm wide strips.

2

Chop the 110 gram onions (peeled) finely.

### Cook

3

Heat the 4 tbsp ghee (or vegetable oil) in a fairly large pot over a medium-high flame. When hot, put in the 110 gram onions . Stir and fry for 3 minutes .

4

Now put in the chopped 1 kg spinach , green chilli (finely chopped), 1 tsp fresh ginger (peeled and very finely grated), 1 tsp salt , and 0.50 tsp sugar . Stir and cook for 5 minutes .

5

Pour in the 125 ml water and bring to a simmer. Cover tightly, reduce the heat to low and cook for 10 minutes . Uncover and boil away some of the extra liquid.

### Finish

6

Sprinkle the 0.25 tsp garam masala over the top and mix.
