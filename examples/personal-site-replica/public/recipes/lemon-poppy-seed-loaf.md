# Lemon Poppy Seed Loaf

A tangy and sweet loaf with a delightful crunch from poppy seeds.

60 minutes cook 20 minutes prep 1 hour 20 minutes total 1 serving

bread loaf lemon poppy seed baking dessert

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2.50 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 0.50 tsp baking powder
- 0.50 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp poppy seeds
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 0.50 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 3 eggs
- 2 tbsp lemon zest
- 0.25 cup fresh lemon juice
- \- powdered sugar with a little
- \- fresh lemon juice to create a glaze

## Equipment

- oven
- loaf-pan
- bowl
- wire-rack

## Instructions

### Prepare Oven

1

Preheat your oven to 350 °F (175°C). Grease your 9x5-inch loaf-pan or line it with parchment paper.

### Dry Ingredients

2

In a large bowl , whisk together the 2.50 cups all-purpose flour , 1 tsp baking soda , 0.50 tsp baking powder , and 0.50 tsp salt . Stir in the 2 tbsp poppy seeds . Set aside.

### Wet Ingredients

3

In another large bowl , beat the 1 cup granulated sugar , 0.50 cup brown sugar packed, and 1 cup vegetable oil until well combined.

4

Beat in the 3 eggs one at a time. Mix in the 2 tbsp lemon zest and 0.25 cup fresh lemon juice until fully incorporated.

### Combine and Bake

5

Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, stirring until just combined.

Note Do not overmix or the loaf will be tough.

6

Pour the batter into the prepared loaf-pan and smooth the top. Bake for 50-60 minutes , or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

### Cool

7

Allow the loaf to cool in the pan for 10 minutes before transferring it to a wire-rack to cool completely.

### Optional Glaze

8

If desired, mix powdered sugar with a little fresh lemon juice to create a glaze . Drizzle over the cooled loaf. Slice and serve.
