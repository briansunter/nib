# Sticky Buns

Soft, milk- and butter-enriched buns stuffed with aromatic cinnamon filling and glazed with a sticky, sugary topping loaded with pecans.

25 minutes cook 25 minutes prep 3 hours 50 minutes total 16 servings

breakfast sticky-buns baking cinnamon pecans

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2.50 tsp instant yeast
- 1 cup water
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 6 tbsp unsalted butter
- 3 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1.25 tsp table salt
- 0.25 cup dry milk
- 0.50 cup dried potato flakes
- 0.50 cup light corn syrup
- 1 tbsp rum
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 cup light brown sugar
- 1 cup pecans
- 0.50 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour

## Equipment

- bowl
- cake pans
- serrated knife
- oven
- cooling rack

## Instructions

### Dough

1

Mix and knead 2.50 tsp instant yeast , 1 cup water lukewarm, 3 cups all-purpose flour , 6 tbsp unsalted butter at room temperature, 3 tbsp granulated sugar , 1.25 tsp table salt , 0.25 cup dry milk , and 0.50 cup dried potato flakes together by hand, mixer, or bread machine set on the dough cycle until a smooth dough forms. If kneading in a stand mixer, it should take 5-7 minutes at second speed, and the dough should barely clean the sides of the bowl, perhaps sticking a bit at the bottom.

Note Use the lesser amount of water in summer or in a humid environment, the greater amount in winter or in a dry climate, and somewhere in between the rest of the year.

2

Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl . Cover and allow to rise at room temperature for 1-2 hours or until nearly doubled in bulk.

3

While the dough is rising, lightly grease two 9-inch round cake pans .

### Glaze

4

Whisk together 0.50 cup light corn syrup , 1 tbsp rum optional, and 3 tbsp butter melted.

5

Divide the glaze evenly between the cake pans . Sprinkle 1 cup light brown sugar packed and 1 cup pecans diced atop the glaze.

### Cinnamon Filling

6

Whisk together 0.50 cup granulated sugar , 2 tsp cinnamon , and 1 tbsp all-purpose flour .

Note Alternatively, use 0.5 cup Baker's Cinnamon Filling mixed with 2 tbsp water.

### Assembly

7

Transfer the dough to a lightly greased work surface, and pat or roll it into a 16 x 12 inch rectangle.

8

Sprinkle filling evenly over the dough, leaving an uncovered strip about 1 inch wide along one long end.

9

Starting with the sugar-covered edge, roll the dough into a log the long way.

10

Using a serrated knife , slice the log into 16 slices.

Note Rinse the blade in hot water and wipe it off between slices to cut down on drag.

11

Space eight buns in each of the prepared cake pans . Cover the pans and let the buns rise until they spread out and touch one another, about 60-90 minutes .

12

While the buns are rising, preheat the oven to 350F.

### Bake

13

Bake the sticky buns for 15 minutes . Tent them lightly with aluminum foil, and bake until they are a light golden brown, about 10 minutes more.

14

Remove the buns from the oven , and loosen their edges with a knife. Carefully turn them out onto a cooling rack or parchment-covered sheet pan to cool, scraping any glaze that may have stuck to the pan onto the warm buns.

Note The sugar is hot, be careful when turning out.

Note Be sure to turn the buns out of their pans immediately. If you wait, the sticky topping will remain in the pan, not on the buns.

15

Serve warm or at room temperature. Wrap airtight and store at room temperature for several days; freeze for longer storage.

Note Don't like nuts? Leave out the pecans.

Note When making anything with yeast, let the dough rise to the point the recipe says it should. Rising times are only a guide; there are so many variables in yeast baking that it's impossible to say that dough will always double in bulk in a specific amount of time.

Note Want to make these fresh for breakfast? Make the dough, shape the buns, and place them in the pan with the glaze; don't let them rise. Place the pan in the refrigerator for up to 16 hours. Remove from the fridge and let rest at room temperature for 1 hour. Bake in a preheated 350F oven for 35 minutes until golden brown and the filling is bubbly.
