# Sous Vide Rosemary Garlic Chicken

This chicken recipe is really simple and healthy but has a ton of flavor from the garlic and rosemary infused by the sous vide.

1h 3m

main sous-vide chicken

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken
- \- seasoned salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp seasoned salt
- 4 cloves minced garlic
- 4 sprigs fresh rosemary

## Equipment

- sous vide bag
- sous vide

## Instructions

1

Prepare a sous vide bag

2

Cover 1 lb chicken with seasoned salt

3

Add chicken to sous vide bag along with 1 tbsp olive oil , 1 tbsp sugar , 2 tbsp seasoned salt , 4 cloves minced garlic and 4 sprigs fresh rosemary

4

Seal the chicken in the bag and put in sous vide at 145 degrees F (62 C) for 1 hour .

5

When a few minutes are left on the timer, heat skillet over medium-high heat.

6

Remove chicken from bag and sear using skillet for 3 minutes on each side.
