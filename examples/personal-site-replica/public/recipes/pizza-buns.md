# Pizza Buns

Savory buns with pizza filling, perfect for parties, picnics, or snacking on the go.

30 minutes cook 25 minutes prep 2 hours 55 minutes total 12 servings

bread pizza buns party snack

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1.25 cups milk
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1.50 tsp salt
- 3.50 cups bread flour
- 2.50 tsp instant yeast
- 0.50 cup pizza sauce
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 cup pepperoni

## Equipment

- stand
- baking
- oven

## Instructions

### Dough

1

Place 1.25 cups milk , 3 tbsp olive oil , 1.50 tsp salt , 3.50 cups bread flour , and 2.50 tsp instant yeast into a bowl. Mix and knead by hand, using a stand mixer, or in a bread machine to make a smooth, soft dough.

2

Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and allow to rise until doubled, 60-90 minutes .

### Assembly

3

Gently deflate the dough and transfer to a lightly greased work surface. Roll the dough into a 12 " x 18 " rectangle.

4

Spread evenly with 0.50 cup pizza sauce , 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese , and 1 cup pepperoni chopped.

Note Feel free to replace the pepperoni with about 1 cup of your favorite cooked pizza toppings. Chop chunky toppings like sliced mushrooms or peppers rather than leaving them whole.

5

Starting with a long end, roll the dough into a log. Cut the log into 12 to 18 pieces depending on how large you want the buns.

6

Space the rounds on two baking sheets lined with parchment or lightly greased. If you've cut 12 rounds, flatten them gently to about 1 " tall.

7

Cover the pans and allow the buns to rise 60-90 minutes until nicely puffed. Towards the end of the rising time, preheat the oven to 350°F.

### Bake

8

Bake the buns for 25-30 minutes until light golden brown.

9

Remove from the oven and serve warm with additional pizza sauce on the side if desired.

Note Store leftovers in the refrigerator. Reheat for about 10 minutes, covered, in a 350°F oven or briefly in the microwave.
