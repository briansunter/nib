# Sushi Rice

Seasoned Japanese rice with vinegar, sugar, and salt for sushi

30 minutes 4 servings

side japanese rice sushi

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 4 cups steamed rice

## Equipment

- bowl

## Instructions

1

Combine 3 tablespoons rice vinegar , 2 tablespoons sugar , and 1 tablespoon kosher salt in a small bowl . Whisk to dissolve.

2

Place 4 cups steamed rice in a large bowl .

3

Pour the vinegar mixture over the hot rice a little bit at a time, gently turning and mixing the rice with a cutting motion. Fan the rice while mixing to cool it and give it a glossy finish.

4

Use immediately for sushi, or cover with a damp cloth until ready to use.
