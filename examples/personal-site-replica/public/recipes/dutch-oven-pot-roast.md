# Dutch Oven Pot Roast

A classic pot roast with deep flavor from proper browning and slow braising. Salt the meat ahead of time, get a good crust on the beef and onions, and braise until fork-tender with liquid only halfway up the meat.

3h 11m

main beef comfort-food dutch-oven braise

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 3 to 4 lb beef chuck roast
- \- kosher salt
- \- black pepper
- 2 to 3 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 large onions
- 4 to 6 cloves garlic
- 1 to 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 2 to 2.5 cups beef stock
- 1 to 2 tbsp worcestershire sauce
- 3 to 4 rosemary sprigs
- 3 to 4 thyme sprigs
- 2 bay leaves
- 1.50 lb carrots
- 1.50 lb small potatoes
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp flour

## Equipment

- oven
- dutch

## Instructions

1

The night before or 30 minutes ahead, salt 3 to 4 lb beef chuck roast generously with kosher salt and black pepper . For best results, refrigerate uncovered for 8-24 hours.

2

Preheat oven to 275 °F to 300°F.

3

Heat 2 to 3 tbsp neutral oil in a dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering. Sear the chuck roast on all sides until a dark crust forms. Let it develop color without moving it too much. Remove to a plate.

Note Cut the roast into 2 large chunks before searing if it's very thick for more surface area.

4

Add 2 large onions , cut into thick wedges, to the pot. Cook until deeply golden. Add 4 to 6 cloves garlic , minced, and cook for 1 minute .

5

Stir in 1 to 2 tbsp tomato paste and cook for 30-60 seconds.

6

Add 1 cup dry red wine and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Add 2 to 2.5 cups beef stock , 1 to 2 tbsp worcestershire sauce , 3 to 4 rosemary sprigs , 3 to 4 thyme sprigs , and 2 bay leaves .

7

Return the beef to the pot. Add enough stock so liquid comes about halfway up the meat. Cover tightly.

8

Braise in oven for 1.5 hours . At this point, add 1.50 lb carrots cut chunky and 1.50 lb small potatoes (or big chunks). Re-cover and continue cooking for another 1.5-2 hours .

Note Keep the braise gentle (275-300°F) for proper collagen-to-gelatin conversion.

9

Total braising time: 3-5 hours depending on size, until a fork slides in easily. Avoid opening the lid frequently. If it's not tender, continue cooking.

10

Remove roast and vegetables to a serving platter. Let the meat rest for 10-20 minutes . Slice against the grain or shred.

11

For gravy: Simmer the remaining liquid in the pot. Whisk in a paste of 1 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp flour a bit at a time until glossy and thick.
