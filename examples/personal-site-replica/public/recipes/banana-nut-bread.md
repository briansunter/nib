# Banana Nut Bread

Classic banana nut bread with toasted pecans

1 hour 8 servings

bread baking banana dessert

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 stick butter
- 1 cup white sugar
- 0.50 cup brown sugar
- 3 bananas
- 0.50 lemon
- 0.25 cup milk
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1.75 cups flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 pinch salt
- 0.50 cup pecans

## Instructions

1

Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F.

2

Cream 1 stick butter and 1 cup white sugar and 0.50 cup brown sugar together.

3

Mix in 3 bananas and 0.50 lemon juice.

4

Add 0.25 cup milk and 1 tsp vanilla .

5

Sift 1.75 cups flour , 1 tsp baking soda , and 1 pinch salt together. Add to wet ingredients.

6

Fold in 0.50 cup pecans .

7

Pour into a greased loaf pan and bake for 60 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
