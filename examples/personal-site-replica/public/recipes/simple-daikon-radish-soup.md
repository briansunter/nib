# Simple Daikon Radish Soup

Easy and delicious soup with tender daikon radish in a flavorful kombu dashi broth.

15 min 4 servings

soup japanese vegan daikon dashi

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 300 gram daikon radish
- \- long onion
- 1200 ml kombu dashi
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 0.50 tbsp salt
- \- pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp sesame oil

## Equipment

- pot

## Instructions

### Prepare the Vegetables

1

Slice the 300 gram daikon radish into small pieces.

2

Slice the long onion into small pieces.

### Cook the Daikon

3

In a large pot , combine the 1200 ml kombu dashi , 1 clove garlic grated, and daikon slices.

4

Simmer for 15 minutes or until the daikon is soft.

### Season the Soup

5

Add 1 tbsp soy sauce , 0.50 tbsp salt , and pepper to taste .

### Finish the Soup

6

Add the sliced long onion and simmer for a few more minutes.

7

Stir in the 1 tbsp sesame oil just before serving.

Note For a vegan-friendly version, make sure to use a kombu-based dashi powder or make your own kombu dashi from scratch. The simplicity of this recipe allows the natural flavor of the daikon to shine through, making it a perfect light meal or starter.
