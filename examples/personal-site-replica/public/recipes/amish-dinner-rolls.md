# Amish Dinner Rolls

Dense yet tender, moist soft dinner rolls made with mashed potato. They stay fresh at room temperature for several days, making them great for make-ahead.

25 minutes cook 15 minutes prep 3 hours 35 minutes total 16 servings

bread rolls baking dinner make-ahead

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 8.50 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 large eggs
- 0.33 cup granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons table salt
- 6 tablespoons butter
- 1 cup mashed potatoes
- 2.50 teaspoons instant yeast
- 0.75 cup lukewarm water
- \- butter

## Equipment

- bowl
- baking pan
- cooling rack

## About

Inspired by a small handwritten Amish cookbook. Mashed potato gives these rolls their soft texture and staying power: they'll remain moist and fresh-tasting at room temperature for several days. One medium-to-large baking potato will yield about 227g of mashed potato.

## Instructions

### Make the Dough

1

Weigh your 4.25 cups all-purpose flour ; or measure it by gently spooning it into a cup, then sweeping off any excess.

2

In a bowl , mix and knead together 2 large eggs , 0.33 cup granulated sugar , 2 teaspoons table salt , 6 tablespoons butter , 1 cup mashed potatoes , 2.50 teaspoons instant yeast , 0.75 cup lukewarm water , and the 4.25 cups all-purpose flour , by hand, mixer, or bread machine set on the dough cycle, to make a smooth, soft dough.

Note If possible, use the water in which the potatoes were boiled.

### First Rise

3

Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl , cover with plastic wrap, and let the dough rise until doubled in bulk, about 90 minutes .

Note If using a bread machine, allow the machine to complete its cycle, then leave the dough in the machine until doubled, perhaps an additional 30 minutes.

### Shape the Rolls

4

Gently deflate the dough and divide it into 16 large balls or 24 smaller balls. Round each ball into a smooth roll.

5

Place the rolls in a lightly greased 9 " x 13 " baking pan . Cover with lightly greased plastic wrap.

### Second Rise

6

Let the rolls rise until quite puffy, 1.5-2 hours . Towards the end of the rising time, preheat the oven to 350°F.

### Bake

7

Bake the rolls for 20-25 minutes , until golden brown and feel set. Remove from the oven and turn out of the pan onto a cooling rack . Brush with melted butter if desired.

Note Bake the rolls rather tightly spaced in a 9" x 13" pan so they crowd each other and become pull-apart rolls with unbrowned sides. For round rolls browned all over, place them in a larger pan farther apart.

### Serve

8

Serve rolls warm or at room temperature. Store well wrapped in plastic for several days at room temperature; freeze for longer storage.
