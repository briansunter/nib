# Oatmeal

Stovetop oatmeal with oatmilk and almond milk

5 min 2 servings

breakfast oatmeal healthy

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 cup oatmilk
- 2 cups almond milk
- 1 cup rolled oats
- \- banana

## Equipment

- saucepan

## Instructions

1

Combine 1 cup oatmilk and 2 cups almond milk in a saucepan . Bring to a simmer over medium heat.

2

Stir in 1 cup rolled oats . Cook for 5 minutes , stirring occasionally, until thickened.

3

Remove from heat. Top with banana (sliced) and any desired toppings.
