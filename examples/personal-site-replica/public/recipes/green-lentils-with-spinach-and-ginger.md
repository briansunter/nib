# Whole Green Lentils with Spinach and Ginger

A very nourishing dish of whole green lentils simmered with spinach, ginger, and fresh coriander.

1 hour 6 servings

main lentils spinach vegetarian indian

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 200 g whole green lentils
- 720 ml water
- 6 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1-2 fresh hot green chillies
- 1 tsp fresh ginger
- 8 tbsp fresh green coriander
- 560 g fresh spinach
- 2 tsp salt
- to taste black pepper
- 2 tbsp lemon juice

## Equipment

- heavy pot
- pot

## Instructions

### Cook the Lentils

1

Put the 200 g whole green lentils and the 720 ml water into a heavy pot and bring to the boil. Cover, reduce the heat to low and simmer gently for 45-60 minutes .

### Prepare the Spinach

2

Over a medium flame, heat the 6 tbsp vegetable oil in a pot large enough to hold the spinach. When hot, put in the 1-2 fresh hot green chillies , finely sliced, and the 1 tsp fresh ginger , peeled and very finely grated. Stir and fry for 10 seconds .

3

Add the 8 tbsp fresh green coriander , chopped, and the 560 g fresh spinach , trimmed, washed and chopped. Stir and cook until the spinach has wilted.

### Combine and Simmer

4

Now put in the cooked lentils and the 2 tsp salt , or to taste. Stir to mix and bring to a simmer. Cover and cook very gently for 10-15 minutes .

### Finish

5

Add the to taste black pepper and 2 tbsp lemon juice , or more to taste. Stir to mix, then cook, uncovered, for another 5 minutes . Check the seasonings and serve.

Note This very nourishing dish goes well with Beef baked with yogurt and black pepper and Lamb dopiaza.
