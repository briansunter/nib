# Green Bean Casserole

Classic green bean casserole with cream of mushroom soup, cheddar cheese, and crispy fried onions.

## Ingredients

- 2 cans green beans
- 1 can condensed cream of mushroom soup
- 1 cup cheddar cheese
- 1 can French-fried onions

## Equipment

- oven
- microwave-safe bowl
- casserole dish

## Instructions

1

Preheat the oven to 350 °F (175°C).

### Mix

2

Mix 2 cans green beans (14.5 ounce cans, drained) and 1 can condensed cream of mushroom soup (10.5 ounce can) together in a large microwave-safe bowl until well combined.

3

Microwave on high until warm, 3 minutes to 5 minutes .

4

Stir 0.50 cup cheddar cheese into the green bean mixture.

5

Microwave on high for 2 minutes to 3 minutes .

### Assemble and Bake

6

Transfer mixture to a casserole dish and spread evenly over the bottom. Sprinkle remaining 0.50 cup cheddar cheese over top, then sprinkle with 1 can French-fried onions (6 ounce can).

7

Bake in the preheated oven until cheese is melted and the onions are just turning brown, 10 minutes .

Note To reheat: Remove the casserole from the fridge and let it come to room temperature for about 30 minutes. Top with French-fried onions and reheat in an oven preheated to 350°F until the onions are golden brown and the casserole is heated through.
