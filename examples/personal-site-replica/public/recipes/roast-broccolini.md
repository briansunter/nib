# Roast Broccolini

Oven-roasted broccolini with garlic, an easy, nutritious 25-minute side dish.

15 minutes cook 10 minutes prep 25 minutes total 4 servings

vegetable broccolini side-dish roasted

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 2 bunches broccolini
- 2.50 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 0.50 tsp kosher salt
- 0.25 tsp black pepper

## Equipment

- oven
- sheet pan

## Instructions

1

Preheat the oven to 425 °F (220°C).

2

Trim the woody ends from 2 bunches broccolini (baby broccoli, just shy of 1 pound) and arrange on a sheet pan .

3

Drizzle with 2.50 tbsp extra virgin olive oil (do not skimp or it may burn) and scatter over smashed and peeled 3 cloves garlic . Season with 0.50 tsp kosher salt and 0.25 tsp black pepper , then toss to coat evenly.

4

Roast for 15 minutes , until the stems are tender-crisp and the tips are deeply browned.

5

Serve hot with an extra pinch of salt.
