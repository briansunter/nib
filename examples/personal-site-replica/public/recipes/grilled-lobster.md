# Grilled Lobster Tails

Buttery Cajun-grilled lobster tails with garlic herb sauce.

15 minutes cook 10 minutes prep 25 minutes total 2 servings

main lobster grilling seafood cajun

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 6 lobster tails
- 2 skewers
- 3 tsp Cajun seasoning
- 6 tbsp butter
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley
- \- lemon

## Equipment

- kitchen shears
- skewers
- grill
- basting brush

## Instructions

### Equipment Needed

1

Gather your kitchen shears , skewers , grill , and a basting brush .

2

Cut down the top of the 2 lobster tails shell using your kitchen shears .

3

Spread the shell open to expose the meat.

4

Flip the 2 lobster tails over.

5

Insert a 2 skewers under the skin to prevent curling during cooking.

6

Season the outside with 2 tsp Cajun seasoning .

7

Combine 4 tbsp butter , 3 cloves garlic (minced), 1 tsp Cajun seasoning , and 1 tbsp olive oil .

8

Heat until melted and well combined.

### Grilling

9

Preheat your grill to 400 degrees F (direct heat).

10

Place the 2 lobster tails meat-side down for a 3 minutes until nicely charred.

11

Flip the tails over.

12

Brush the melted sauce mixture on top.

Note Be careful of flare-ups from the butter.

13

Cook until internal temperature reaches 140 degrees F, about 5 minutes .

### Serving

14

Remove from grill .

15

Top with 2 tbsp fresh parsley (chopped).

16

Drizzle with additional 2 tbsp butter sauce.

17

Garnish with lemon (cut into wedges).

18

Serve immediately.

Note These lobster tails were purchased at Mariano's and cooked on a Big Green Egg grill.
