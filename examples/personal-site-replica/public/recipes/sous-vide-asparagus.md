# Sous Vide Asparagus

Sous Vide Asparagus

10 min 4 servings

sous-vide asparagus vegetables side-dish

Scale

Units

## Ingredients

- 1 bunch asparagus
- 0.50 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- \- lemon
- \- sea salt

## Equipment

- sous vide immersion circulator

## Instructions

1

Preheat sous vide immersion circulator to 185°F.

2

Place 1 bunch asparagus , 0.50 tsp black pepper , 2 tbsp olive oil , and the zest and juice of the lemon in a vacuum bag. Seal according to manufacturer's instructions.

3

Cook in the water bath for 10 minutes until tender-crisp.

4

Remove from bag, season with sea salt and serve immediately.
